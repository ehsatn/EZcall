Your Android calls and texts on a second phone

Answer calls, send texts and look up contacts on a nearby iPhone or Android phone using the SIM in your sending phone.

Download Pair your phones

What can you share?

Calls

See incoming calls on the second phone. Enable call control on the Sender to answer, decline or dial through the Android SIM.

Text messages

Read incoming SMS and sync the latest 100 messages. Enable SMS sending on the Sender to reply or start a conversation using its SIM.

Contacts

With your permission, ezcall copies names and phone numbers from the Sender into the receiving app.

A direct, encrypted connection

The paired phones exchange data over end-to-end encrypted Bluetooth. No relay server handles your calls, messages or contacts.

Pair your phones

  1. 1

    Install the apps

    Install Sender on the Android phone with the SIM. On the second phone, install Receiver for Android 12+ or use ezcall for iPhone with iOS 17+.

  2. 2

    Scan the pairing QR

    Enable Bluetooth on both phones. Tap Start pairing on the Sender. On the receiver, choose Scan QR (Android) or Scan QR code (iPhone). Keep both apps open.

  3. 3

    Test the connection

    Tap Test connection on the receiver. Choose which call, SMS and contact features to share on the Sender and grant their individual permissions.

Set up Wireless debuggingOptional · On the sending Android phone

For call audio on the receiver, pair ezcall with the debugging service on the sending Android phone itself. The two phones still pair by QR and exchange data over Bluetooth.

  1. Connect the Sender to Wi-Fi and turn on Developer options. On most phones, open About phone and tap Build number seven times.
  2. In ezcall, tap Pair with Wireless debugging. Allow notifications if prompted.
  3. Open Developer options → Wireless debugging, enable it and select Pair device with pairing code. Keep the code dialog open.
  4. In the notification shade, tap Enter code on the ezcall notification and enter the six digits. If port discovery fails, enter the code and port from the same dialog in the form 123456:37811.

Under Call audio on the receiving phone, tap Set up call audio. The app reports whether the handset supports two-way or listen-only audio. To revoke authorization, remove ezcall from Paired devices in Wireless debugging.

Frequently asked questions

Devices and connection

Which phones can send and receive?

Use Android 12+ with BLE peripheral advertising as the Sender. Use an iPhone with iOS 17+ or a second Android phone with Android 12+ as the receiver.

How do I connect the phones?

Enable Bluetooth, open the pairing QR on the Sender and scan it inside the receiver. Run Test connection on the receiver. Pairing steps · Wireless debugging guide

Do I need internet or Wi-Fi?

Pairing and everyday relay use Bluetooth without an internet account. The optional call-audio setup uses Wi-Fi for Wireless debugging on the Sender.

What happens outside Bluetooth range?

The relay tries to reconnect when the phones are nearby again. Call alerts require a live connection while the phone rings. You can sync recent SMS after reconnecting.

Calls, texts and privacy

Does the receiving phone need a SIM?

Only the Android Sender needs an active SIM for carrier calls and texts.

Can I answer a call on the receiver?

Enable Call control on the Sender to answer, decline or dial. For audio on the receiver, complete the call-audio setup. Two-way audio depends on the Android handset. In listen-only mode, speak into the Sender.

How are calls and messages protected?

The pairing QR authenticates the devices. Every session uses fresh keys and AES-256-GCM encryption. Show the QR only to a phone you trust.

Can I transfer files?

ezcall supports calls, SMS and contact sync. File transfer is outside its supported features.
